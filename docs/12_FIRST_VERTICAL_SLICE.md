# AQIL — First Complete End-to-End Vertical Slice

**Sequencing:** Build **`docs/07_ROADMAP_AND_GATES.md` § M1** (macro engine pipeline) first. This document describes the **later** full reader-to-evaluation slice (G4-class), not the immediate next sprint.

## Outcome
A real official economic/policy change becomes an accessible source-backed article and a frozen research hypothesis **without** pretending we already know its investment consequences. Any virtual portfolio test is only registered if fund data quality, market pricing and a falsifiable exposure claim pass all gates.

## Scope (do not enlarge during first slice)
- One relevant policy/event source and one corrected or amended version; one macro indicator with first-release/revision dates.
- One coherent causal path of 3–5 edges and one meaningful opposing mechanism.
- One well-documented existing method or rules-only comparator, not a new AI trading formula.
- Zero to two conditional ETF examples after verified instrument identity and exposure; **zero is a valid result**.
- One registered research hypothesis plus a status of `insufficient_evidence` if no meaningful forecast possible.
- One accessible Today short read, connected analysis and method/audit view driven by one structured evidence record.
- One predeclared synthetic/dry-run virtual lab strategy and one flow-matched passive benchmark; real future performance remains pending.

## The user path
1. A person opens Today and reads a headline that states the observed change but not an invented conclusion.
2. In under 90 seconds, they understand a credible possible consequence and the leading uncertainty.
3. They follow a related economic tangent without losing the path back.
4. They open Full Research to find the exact government/statistical source, vintage, method card and alternatives.
5. If investable data pass, they inspect broad exposure implications and see why holding a global ETF might already capture part of the theme.
6. They read the registered hypothesis with explicit future measurement and can return later for accuracy and any post-mortem.
7. If the source is corrected, the story visibly receives a correction and the research dependency graph quarantines compromised claims.

## Demo acceptance (all must pass before calling slice complete)
- One source with unambiguous primary URL and exact affected excerpt/field.
- History query at an earlier date does not contain a later revision.
- Numeric values are parsed from real source or plainly marked as synthetic, never generated ad hoc.
- Agent-produced false number/claim citation fails review.
- Duplicate news story appears once as an origin cluster.
- Authoritative but ambiguous policy uses 'announced/appropriated/implemented' correctly.
- Quick read and deep study share a research version and are substantively consistent.
- Missing ETF data means no eligible product recommendation.
- Virtual order cannot use an unavailable close and duplicate job is idempotent.
- Original forecast is frozen; review and corrected interpretation can only append versions.
- Novice explains the message and uncertainty; expert traces methodology; keyboard/mobile checks pass.
- Costs, data rights, freshness, health and failed jobs visible in internal operator panel.

## The first review decision
If we cannot produce a reliable and sufficiently useful *single* source-to-reader-to-evaluation research slice at reasonable cost, stop increasing scope. Investigate source quality, reasoning and UX until that works. Multiplying 1,027 factors or adding agents before this gate would magnify failure, not improve the product.
