# AQIL — Governance, Public Accountability, Pricing and Regulatory Readiness

## Scientific/editorial independence
- Aqil is a research publication and analytical tool with human accountability, not an all-knowing authority. Distinguish objective records, model calculations, editor interpretation and educational guidance.
- Published material research hypotheses are preregistered; editor cannot retrospectively rewrite or delete a losing forecast to improve a published scorecard.
- Sources and primary methodological references linked with observed dates, methodological versions, licensed-use status and uncertainty.
- Criticism is accepted through structured `peer_challenge`: challenged claim, alternative source/reason, severity, status, editor response, outcome. No automatic publication of unverified challenge content.
- A legitimate correction includes original publication, date discovered, nature/severity of error, affected conclusions, what remains unaffected and any portfolio experiment invalidations. 'We cannot determine why' is an acceptable conclusion.
- Conflicts register lists provider commissions, sponsorship, affiliate agreements, analyst compensation, owner exposures (where disclosure relevant), material third-party editorial arrangements. Editorial ranking criteria never secretly optimise affiliate revenue.
- Public research performance shows ALL relevant completed hypotheses including failures, full denominator, inception date, methodology shifts, excluded samples, costs, FX and uncertainty. No chart cherry-picking.

## Governance roles (solo at launch; separation by process)
- Founder = product owner and initial human reviewer; self-review has limitations clearly disclosed.
- Data integrity = deterministic validators / fixtures, independently checked before publication of new source types.
- Research reviewer = signs off causal and numerical claims; eventually reviewer diversity/peer review process.
- Model evaluator = test suite, frozen performance and challenger criteria; code must separate scoring from proposal.
- Security/operations = release approval and incident management, even when roles belong to the same founder initially.
- User/customer = retains agency; research is information, not obligation to act.

## Founder lifetime access promise (design contract)
Until precisely published: internal plan, **not yet a public customer promise**. If public promise made:
- Establish UTC cutoff timestamp and account verification requirements before announcement.
- Issue durable `founding_lifetime` entitlement on eligibility; preserve original entitlement record and versioned scope.
- Promise should say **free while Aqil is operated**, define covered features, whether future major premium add-ons included, and reasonable nontransferability and anti-abuse terms. Avoid 'all future features forever' without willing to honour it.
- Existing accounts created before cutoff should not have to subscribe or requalify on payment transition; check access in database auth middleware and test across upgrades.
- Record fair-use/rate limit policy equally; ban/suspend only pursuant to clear rules and appeal process. Do not silently downgrade founding cohort.
- Assess economics at estimated 100/1,000/10,000 founding users; free signup floods, bot abuse, provider per-seat data rights and model cost are risks.

## Commercial product constraints
- Phase A private personal use. Phase B free educational/research multiuser after privacy, copyright, RLS and editorial readiness. Phase C subscriptions after independent legal/regulatory review, payments security, consumer terms, operational and cost feasibility.
- Distinguish general research from personalised financial recommendations, model portfolios, portfolio-specific reallocation and trade execution. A financial-advice disclaimer does not exempt regulated conduct. Obtain Singapore-qualified advice on MAS licensing/perimeter prior to launching personal recommendations, even if the engine calls them 'insights'. Additional jurisdictions separately assessed.
- Privacy obligations under Singapore PDPA: clear notification/consent where required, accuracy, access/correction, protection, retention, transfers, breach response, DPO and processor controls. Verify current legal requirements before deployment.
- Third-party market-data licences must explicitly cover storage, private view, commercial articles and subscriber display. Publisher partnerships, press extracts and AI training/data-use rights separate questions.
- Prefer transparent pricing if paying service launched. No paywall over important corrections to previously freely distributed research; public accountability should remain accessible even if detailed tools become paid.

## Education and suitability ethics
- Never tell all users to invest a fixed share of income regardless of debt, income volatility, liquidity or ability to absorb drawdowns.
- Explain risks in the same visual layer as potential compounding results; show nominal/real, contributions/growth, downside and fee sensitivity. For university fees or house deposit within a few years, explain why equities may be unsuitable for near-term essential spending.
- Product referral disclosures include compensation, geography, tax, costs, account minimums, legal entity, investor protections and data-link permissions. No 'best broker' guarantee.
- Users can use Aqil completely without linking portfolio or giving sensitive financial data. Public Today/Explore/Learn never need investment-account access.

## Data deletion vs append-only academic history
Original public forecasts and methods remain immutable; they use no personal identifiers or raw user's private ledger. User private data has correct access, export/retention/deletion pathways; minimise contents in model prompts and backups. 'Immutable' must not become an excuse to retain private data contrary to applicable law or promises.

## Editorial release standards
- For consequential claims: primary evidence or clearly bounded corroborated secondary evidence; explicit observations vs interpretations; valid source IDs and licences; no fabricated numbers; source freshness; opposing view considered.
- All high-impact financial, policy or safety claims initially need human review, even if syntactic validators pass.
- Public article labels: `Explainer`, `Developing`, `Research`, `Forecast registered`, `Evaluated`, `Correction`.
- Correct a material claim at both the research source and every consumed story. Don't quietly edit without history.
- The platform does not claim regulated status, accreditation, academic peer review or tested alpha before substantiation.

## Definition of subscription-readiness
Only launch subscriptions after verified customer utility, reliable source pipeline and rights, 90-day operational metrics with incidents addressed (illustrative internal target, not mandatory legal threshold), recoverable backups, multi-tenant test, fair terms, privacy notices, regulatory review, payment compliance, and monitoring of founding entitlements. A subscription should fund credible research, not more noise.
