# AQIL — Authoritative Product Charter
**Version 2.0 · 16 October 2026 · Founder-first, scale-aware**

## The promise
**Aqil helps people understand how the world is changing, learn sound investing principles, and build long-horizon wealth through disciplined, evidence-led decisions.** It makes serious investment methods accessible without asking ordinary readers to compile thousands of datapoints, master financial modelling, or trust an AI guru.

**One-line positioning:** *Understand the world. Invest with perspective. Build wealth patiently.*

**Who we are:** Curious, accountable investors applying and testing published research, not omniscient forecasters. We make our assumptions, sources, errors, financial results and improvements inspectable. Peer challenge is welcome. We don't pretend to own an investment discovery just because AI summarised it.

**Who we are not:** A get-rich-quick service, a stock-picking feed, a day-trading terminal, an authority that demands trust, a guaranteed-returns product, or a substitute for personal judgment and professional advice where warranted.

## Product hierarchy: all previously agreed ideas retain their place
1. **Research crown jewel (investment intelligence and causal hypothesis engine).** The primary technical investment: authoritative evidence, proven/published methods, causal hypotheses, alternative scenarios, 12/24/36-month broad-investment outlooks, independently evaluated virtual portfolios, calibrated prediction and historical learning.
2. **World intelligence and editorial reading.** Calm, daily commute-length reading spanning policy, conflict, climate, energy, technology, demography, industry, capital and markets. Read broadly, follow interesting tangents, inspect sources. Daily articles need not create financial recommendations.
3. **Wealth companion.** Private portfolio recording and accurate performance, diversification, contribution discipline, goal scenarios, benchmark and optional long-run educational context. Eventually read-only broker integrations.
4. **Investment education.** Approachable guides to emergency liquidity, fees, risk, broad diversification, real vs nominal returns, ETFs, accumulation, compounding, disciplined saving and behaviour. Never shame low earners or promise five percent of any salary will fund education or retirement.
5. **Peer scrutiny and accountability.** Real methods, corrections, post-mortems, challenger results, user disputes and unresolved questions visible from relevant content.

All five derive from a shared research/data foundation. The interface is calm; the hidden system is demanding.

**Current delivery (22 October 2026).** The five ideas above remain the product, in that order. Investment intelligence is the organising purpose. My Wealth is a supporting pillar: it records what is held so intelligence can later be compared with a real account. Local My Wealth code is currently ahead of live intelligence. That is a sequencing fact, not a change of purpose. Signed-in Today separates a publication from the last 14 days, earlier sourced releases, one stored historical unemployment series and the next confirmed calendar time. The stored releases are Employment Situation and Federal Reserve monetary-policy press items. Intelligence Upcoming shows confirmed times from the official BLS release calendar. None of these is an investment conclusion. Research must not silently change the investor's targets.

The closed loop is: **My Wealth** establishes what is actually held and what it is worth; **Deploy Capital** supports a disciplined decision about new money; **Forward Intelligence** may later inform alternatives; My Wealth then compares the result with benchmarks and frozen counterfactuals. Account-value growth is not investment return. A hypothetical fund-structure saving is not realised profit. A projection is not a guarantee.

Indicative order. Later items are not built:

1. Trustworthy live Saxo portfolio.
2. Secure hosted persistence and historical evidence.
3. Strategic targets and Deploy Capital V1: an approved ETF, a monetary gap, and a reason. No automatic trades.
4. Benchmarks, contributions and long-term planning foundations.
5. Prospective model portfolios and fund-structure attribution.
6. Forward Intelligence only where it can change a real decision, and only with evaluation. The November employment baseline is a prospective-methodology exercise. It does not prove forecasting skill.

## Audience and jobs to be done
- **Curious commuter:** 'Make sense of what matters globally in five minutes, and show the links between apparently unrelated developments.'
- **First-time investor:** 'Show me why patience, low costs, saving and diversification matter, in language I can understand and without assuming spare cash.'
- **Disciplined investor:** 'Help me understand my money, whether I stick to my plan, what I already own and which broad economic changes deserve consideration.'
- **Research challenger:** 'Show sources, dates, model equations and flaws so I can question your interpretation.'
- **Founder:** private daily utility first, scaling-ready mechanics second, paying customers only after usefulness, data rights and legal boundaries are proved.

These are **initial research hypotheses**, not confirmed customer segments. Test with multiple levels of financial literacy and accessibility needs.

## Research product contract
A core research object answers:
1. What exactly changed? **Observed** facts with primary provenance and publication dates.
2. Why might it matter? Explicit mechanisms and alternative causes; direction and lag.
3. What do established methods show? Named, versioned calculations with suitability and limitations.
4. What is the market already anticipating? Direct valuation/consensus evidence or explicit 'unknown'.
5. What are plausible 12-, 24-, 36-month scenarios? Distinct assumptions, counterarguments, falsifiers and evaluation dates; no invented precision.
6. What are the broad allocation implications? No-action first; existing diversification and holdings overlap; structural/strategic alternatives. ETF candidates only after eligibility.
7. How did prior claims perform? Original forecast preserved; accurate/incorrect/inconclusive outcomes and correction history.

**No fabricated or untraceable claim is allowed to reach publication.** Operational reality: perfect truth cannot be guaranteed, so fail closed on unsupported material assertions and make reporting/correction auditable.

## Investment philosophy
- Work, live, save if affordable, control expensive debt, build adequate liquidity, diversify, contribute consistently, keep costs low, value time and compounding; the portfolio is a tool for life goals.
- Research may alter allocation strategically, not only with a 20% tactical sleeve; there is no built-in obligation to change the portfolio.
- Only consider eligible broad diversified, preferably accumulating ETFs with verified ISIN/share class, fee and tracking characteristics, fund and index history, investment exposure and overlap. Regional/factor/sector funds can be conditional rather than treated as 'core'.
- No action, continuing contributions and rebalancing existing holdings are legitimate research outcomes.
- Do not optimise to maximise reader actions, trade frequency, alerts, portfolio turnover or sensational engagement.
- Vary risk guidance by time horizon, liquidity and goals; a child's near-term tuition cannot safely be modelled as a guaranteed equity investment payoff.

## Trust and editorial promise
**Show our work.** Every consequential publication references evidence and the research/methodology version; corrections are linked back; peer challenges can be reviewed; dissent is not hidden merely for contradicting the thesis. Research quality must be distinguishable from persuasive prose.

**No false expertise.** Cite foundational third-party studies. Separate 'published method', 'we replicated it', 'backtest suggests' and 'our frozen forward result shows'. Performance claims include universe, fees, FX, risk, dates, sample size and methodology.

**No forced finance angle.** Major societal/economic stories may conclude with 'no clear investable implication'. Headlines don't become ETF suggestions by default.

## Commercial path
- Phase A: private personal tool, no outside users and no trade execution.
- Phase B: optional free accounts and public reading when security, rights, moderation, privacy and disclosure are ready.
- Phase C: subscription only following demonstrated ongoing utility and a regulatory/consumer-contract review.
- **Founding-user promise:** verified, non-abusive accounts created before a disclosed cutoff get a versioned lifetime-free entitlement while Aqil is operated. The precise feature scope must be stated clearly *before* the promise is marketed. Entitlements live in an audited database, not a date condition buried in UI code. Nontransferability, fair-use, account termination for serious abuse and future material product changes require clearly disclosed terms.
- Potential future broker recommendations: editorial comparisons with compensation/conflicts clearly disclosed; no affiliate-led ranking masquerading as independent research.

## Product experiences
Primary navigation is three destinations: **Today**, **My Wealth** and **Intelligence**.

**Today** is the landing page: one material development, portfolio exceptions, and the next catalyst. It does not invent a feed. **My Wealth** (`/my-wealth`) is private positions, contribution versus growth, allocation, targets and unknowns. **Deploy Capital** is a task inside My Wealth, not a fourth destination. **Intelligence** opens on upcoming catalysts (`/calendar`). Evidence stays at `/research` and vetted exposures at `/explore`, both as section navigation, not separate products. **Learn** (`/learn`) is contextual literacy, linked from the footer, not from the account control. Sign out is the account control until settings exist.

## Success measures
1. Human understanding: can a reader accurately explain the conclusion **and** key uncertainty?
2. Research integrity: consequential facts trace to defensible evidence; no unsupported quantitative outputs; systematic corrections.
3. Method reproducibility: independent rerun reproduces calculations from same data vintages and model versions.
4. Forecast/process quality: calibration, false-positive rate, contradiction coverage, post-mortem completeness, pre-registered experiment survival.
5. Portfolio correctness: accurate ledger, FX, total returns, flow-matched benchmark and clear limitations.
6. Personal benefit: retained financial concepts, use of disciplined savings plan where affordable, voluntary useful reading—not clicks, trading activity or promises of alpha.
7. Economic sustainability: actual serving costs, source/licence rights, support load and security are manageable.

## Hard exclusions at V1
No auto-execution; personalised trade-order prompts; leverage/inverse funds; user-generated trading chat; public leaderboards; claimed verified alpha; invented stock prices/fund metadata; general open-web browsing with uncontrolled agent permissions; unreviewed publication; retroactive changes to forecast records; proprietary sources without permitted use.
